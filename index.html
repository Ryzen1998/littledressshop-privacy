Little Dress Shop – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Little Dress Shop ("the game") is made by Blueline Studio ("we"). The game is made for children aged 6–10 and the grown-ups who play with them. This policy explains what information the game uses and why.

The short version

  • The game has no accounts, no chat and no way for players to contact each other.
  • We do not ask for names, email addresses, photos, contacts or location.
  • Your progress (coins, dresses, the shop name you type) is saved only on your device.
  • The game shows ads. Ads are provided by advertising partners, who receive some device information as described below.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress on the device: coins, finished dresses, settings, the name you give your shop, and the birth year entered on the first screen. This stays on the device, is never sent to us, and is deleted when you uninstall the game.

The age screen

When the game starts for the first time it asks for the player's year of birth. We use this only on the device, to decide how ads are shown. If the player may be under 13, or chooses "not sure", the game treats them as a child: ads are not personalised and the advertising ID is not shared.

Advertising

The game shows ads (for example, an optional video to double coins). We use Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) to show ads, which may work with ad networks such as Unity Ads. To show an ad and count it, these partners may collect:

  • device information (model, operating system, language, screen size);
  • IP address and approximate country;
  • information about the ads shown and tapped;
  • for players who are 13 or older only: the device's advertising ID, which may be used for personalised ads.

For players who may be under 13 we tell every ad partner the ad request is child-directed (COPPA), ask for non-personalised ads only, and do not send the advertising ID. Our ad partners are certified for Google Play's Families program.

You can reset or delete the advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device's settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

Partner privacy policies: ironSource / LevelPlay, Unity Ads.

Purchases

The game may offer optional one-time purchases (for example, extra decorations or removing ads). Payments are handled by Google Play; we never see card or payment details.

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The only information that leaves the device is what our ad partners need to show child-directed, non-personalised ads, as described above. If you believe a child's personal information has been collected, contact us and we will help remove it.

Your choices

  • Uninstalling the game deletes everything it saved.
  • You can limit ad tracking in your device settings.
  • You can contact us with any question or request about your information.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update this page and the date at the top.
